ABOUT BLEU

The story behind the sound

BLEU / DJ & PRODUCER

GROOVE IS THE LANGUAGE

BLEU is a groove-driven DJ and producer whose sound defies boundaries. Rooted in the pulsing rhythms of Tech-House, the raw energy of Jersey Club, the soulful warmth of Amapiano, and the spiritual depth of Afro House — every set BLEU plays is a journey built to move both body and soul.

Born out of a relentless love for the dancefloor, BLEU crafts percussive, rolling sets that hold the crowd from first drop to last record. His productions carry the same weight: warm basslines, layered rhythms, and an undeniable groove that hits every time.

Whether behind the decks at a warehouse rave or in the studio finishing a new mix for Bleu's Radio, BLEU's mission never changes — move the dancefloor.
